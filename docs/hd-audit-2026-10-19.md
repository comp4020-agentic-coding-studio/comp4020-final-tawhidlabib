Eleven auditors (ten Sonnet agents with one area each, plus a Haiku agent
for mechanical checks), led and verified by the Opus lead. Each judged one
area against the course's marking criteria, fetched from the course site
on 19 Oct, and against the industry standard for that area: OWASP Top 10
and ASVS for security, WCAG 2.2 AA for accessibility, Core Web Vitals,

Every finding below marked **verified** was reproduced by the lead, not

### What is already at gold standard (don't change it)

- **Real-time:** p95 483 ms between two people. In a room of **8
  simultaneous viewers**: p50 285 ms, p95 405 ms, zero missed updates.
- **Holds up:**
  - keyboard grid (one tab stop, arrow keys, autosave)
  - a resize mid-tick in both directions
  - a slow connection (one POST, saved in 875 ms)
  - going offline and back (no lost work)
  - two people proposing in the same second (one plan)
  - two taps on the last spot 83 ms apart (one going, one waitlisted)
- **Every page at both viewports:** 0 horizontal overflow, 0 console
  errors, 0 failed requests, 0 broken images.
- **Access control:** every cross-person attempt was refused, across every
  route and role tried. Cross-site POSTs are refused, cookies are
  `HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Lax`, secrets carry 64–192 bits, every text
  field is escaped in every context, and there's no raw SQL.
- **Correctness:**
  - calendar times are right across both DST transitions and in Perth's
    timezone;
  - the blind grid can't be bypassed through any page;
  - waitlist order, admin succession and bans all behave correctly.
- **Accessibility:** a real-browser axe scan found 0 violations on all but
  two rules, across 16 pages × 2 viewports × light/dark × 5 themes. The
  grid is a real table of labelled checkboxes, the tabs follow the ARIA
  pattern, and reduced motion is honoured.
- **Process raw material:**
  - 129 commits in six dated bursts and three crit tags;
  - spec → feat → harness triplets;
  - commit bodies that give cause, repro and red/green
    (`12e9694`, `047d3fa`, `e8026da`, `bc4f8c3`);
  - zero "wip" commits;
  - a 6,000-word prompt history.
- **The idea:** the blind grid is enforced at the source, held by 6 tests
  and one in real Chrome. It's the "pointed, surprising" part of the
  answer.

### 5. Phone keyboard focus hidden under the bottom nav (artefact HD line "the keyboard" · S)

**Verified:** only `scroll-padding-top` is set. At 390×844, tabbing leaves
"Save my hours", grid cells and "Add to Google Calendar" 56–100% under the
fixed nav, failing WCAG 2.4.11. Add
`html { scroll-padding-bottom: calc(nav height + 1rem) }`, and a Chrome
test that tabs through and checks the focused element isn't covered.

### 6. Focus ring too faint (artefact · S)

**Verified:** the ring is the accent at 38% opacity, which measures
1.7–2.5:1 against the page (WCAG 1.4.11 needs 3:1) in every theme and
mode. Use the solid accent, or a two-colour ring.

### 7. Your own second device isn't live (artefact "real-time" · S–M)

**Verified as deliberate** (`src/scripts/live.ts:10`): the page keeps
*your own* hours untouched so unsaved taps survive. The side effect is
that your laptop doesn't show hours you saved on your phone until you
reload. Fix: when a tab has no unsaved taps, adopt your own hours from the
refreshed grid. Add a two-tab test.

### 8. Branded 404, and the 500 (artefact · S)

- **Verified:** there's no `src/pages/404.astro`, so `/nope` shows Astro's
  default page with no way back. Add one in the app's layout.
- **Verified:** `/g/<id>?week=9999-12-27` gives a **500**. Clamp weeks to
  ±10 years, as nudge already does, and add a test.

### 9. Security headers and photo location (security · S + M)

- **Verified: none of the six standard headers are sent.** Add them in
  `src/middleware.ts`: `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` (invite and sign-in
  links are secrets in URLs), `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, HSTS,
  `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `Permissions-Policy`, and a CSP. That takes the
  grade from F to roughly A–B.
- **Verified: uploaded photos keep their EXIF metadata,** including GPS
  (`media.ts:43` writes the bytes as-is). A friend's photo can reveal
  where they live, and avatars are public. Re-encode with `sharp`, pinned
  to 0.35.5 or later, which strips metadata.
- **Verified:** `pnpm audit --prod` reports **17 advisories (11 high)**,
  mostly through Astro. `pnpm update` and overrides fix them; re-run the
  tests after.
